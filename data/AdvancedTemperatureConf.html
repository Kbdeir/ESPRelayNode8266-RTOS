Behavior And Wiring

Sensor 1 on GPIO14 measures the tank. Sensor 2 on GPIO12 measures the panel. Each sensor uses its own data bus and its own 4.7 kOhm pull-up to 3.3 V.

The relay turns ON when the panel is inside the configured range and panel - tank > difference. It turns OFF when the panel is outside the range or panel - tank < difference. Equality keeps the current state.

Enabling this controller also enables DS18B20 sensing and reserves both sensor GPIOs from digital-input use.
